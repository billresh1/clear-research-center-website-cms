What we study

Public institutions under pressure—and in transition.

CLEAR studies how workforce systems, political institutions, organizational behavior, and emerging technologies shape government capacity. Our work joins rigorous social science with public data, practitioner needs, and student learning.

Workforce & expertise

Recruitment, retention, mobility, remote work, skills, and the institutional conditions that attract and sustain public servants.

CLEAR at Georgia State

An Atlanta base for nationally relevant public-service research.

At the Andrew Young School of Policy Studies, CLEAR links a student-centered research enterprise to Atlanta’s dense network of public, nonprofit, civic, and private institutions.

That setting supports experiential learning, community-engaged scholarship, and research partnerships that move from evidence to tools, training, and public impact.

We connect academic research to the real workforce and institutional choices facing public leaders.

William G. Resh, Director of CLEAR
