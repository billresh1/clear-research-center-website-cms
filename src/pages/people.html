A network for intellectual exchange and public impact.

CLEAR connects scholars, students, practitioners, public-interest organizations, and institutional partners around civic leadership, civil service, and government capacity.

Student collaborators

Research experience with a public purpose.

CLEAR integrates students into active projects rather than separating education from research production.

Roles may include data acquisition and documentation, ETL workflows, literature reviews, research design, statistical and network analysis, natural-language processing, visualization, practitioner briefs, and event production.

  • Graduate research assistantships and project teams
  • Capstone and experiential-learning partnerships
  • Doctoral collaboration and coauthorship
  • Applied work with civic and institutional partners
