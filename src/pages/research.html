Research portfolio

How public institutions gain, use, and lose capacity.

CLEAR studies the workforce, institutional, behavioral, technological, and political foundations of effective and accountable government.

Collaborative infrastructure

Accountability and Reform Research Consortium

ARRC is a multi-institutional research network designed to document and explain rapid changes in public administration while building durable data and communities of practice.

Pillar 1

Government capacity

Changes in agency capability, implementation, service delivery, and organizational performance.

Pillar 2

Workforce & expertise

Recruitment, exits, skills, professional knowledge, and the institutions that sustain public service.

Pillar 3

Structural & societal impacts

How administrative change affects sectors, communities, regions, and democratic governance.

Pillar 4

Data & analytical infrastructure

Shared data, documentation, methods, and access systems for cumulative public-interest research.

Methods and collaboration

Bring data, theory, and public problems together.

CLEAR projects use administrative records, job postings, surveys, experiments, qualitative evidence, text analysis, machine learning, network analysis, and quasi-experimental designs.
