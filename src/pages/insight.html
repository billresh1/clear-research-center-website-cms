Workforce analytics platform

INSIGHT+ makes the federal workforce visible.

Integrated Network Solutions in Government Hiring Trends connects personnel, occupations, job postings, geography, institutions, and external labor-market data.

The platform

A common analytic environment for public-service labor markets.

INSIGHT+ transforms fragmented federal workforce data into a documented, extensible infrastructure for research, teaching, and evidence-based workforce strategy.

The project integrates federal personnel and hiring data with occupational classifications, labor-market measures, geographic context, and institutional attributes. Researchers can examine patterns across agencies, occupations, places, time, and career stages rather than relying on isolated snapshots.

What INSIGHT+ enables

Move from description to explanation and foresight.

The infrastructure supports descriptive dashboards, causal research, cross-sector matching, forecasting, and tailored analytical access for different kinds of users.

Monitor workforce change

Track hiring, separations, occupations, grades, agencies, regions, and workforce composition over time.

Evaluate policy shocks

Study shutdowns, remote-work policy, reorganization, political change, and other interventions across agencies.

Connect external context

Match federal data to private labor markets, wages, occupations, geography, ideology, and institutional structure.

Forecast skills and tasks

Connect occupational specifications to emerging AI capabilities and future workforce-development scenarios.

Support practitioners

Translate analysis into data stories, briefings, workforce diagnostics, and decision-relevant visualizations.

Train students

Provide a live environment for data engineering, computational social science, public management, and policy research.

Data pipeline

Designed for reproducible, extensible research.

INSIGHT+ is more than a dashboard. Its value lies in the documented pipeline that converts multiple administrative sources into linked, research-ready data.

Acquire

Ingest personnel, hiring, occupational, geographic, and external labor-market sources.

Transform

Clean, standardize, deduplicate, classify, and harmonize records across time and systems.

Link

Connect agencies, occupations, locations, job text, institutional measures, and outcomes.

Deliver

Serve dashboards, APIs, research extracts, data stories, and teaching applications.

Questions the infrastructure helps answer.

Government shutdowns

Do political disruptions change who joins and leaves government?

Linked recruitment and personnel data measure applications, selections, voluntary exits, and involuntary separations across exposed and unexposed agencies.
